### 🧠 About Me

I'm a passionate Computer Science graduate with a strong inclination towards full-stack development and hardware integration. My interests lie in:

- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Building a mini data center using Proxmox, aiming to integrate it with a web portal for customer VM deployment and billing.
- **Hardware Mastery**: Studying motherboard schematics from phones to computers to gain deep hardware understanding.
- **Real-World Networking**: Hands-on experience with server rack installation, gateway configuration, and domain/network setup during fieldwork at Kasulu Teachers College.
- **Open Source Software**: Actively working on practical systems like Parcel Transportation, e-Pharmacy, and School Management.

---

### 🚀 Projects

Here are some of the projects I've been working on:

- 📦 **[Parcel Transportation System](https://github.com/karimshaban01/parcel_transportation_system)**: Streamlining parcel sending, tracking, and delivery across cities with real-time tracking, role-based access, and performance dashboards.

- 💊 **[e-Pharmacy](https://github.com/karimshaban01/e_pharmacy)**: Bringing pharmacy services online with features like medicine search, prescription uploads, and admin/pharmacist dashboards.

- 🏨 **[Hotel Management System](https://github.com/karimshaban01/hotel_management_system)**: A PHP-based system focusing on booking and room management with separate portals for staff and customers.

- 🏫 **[School Management System](https://github.com/karimshaban01/school_management_system)**: Managing students, teachers, and academic records efficiently.

- 🌍 **[Community Forums](https://github.com/karimshaban01/community_forums)**: A location-based platform encouraging users to discuss ideas and concerns within their communities.
